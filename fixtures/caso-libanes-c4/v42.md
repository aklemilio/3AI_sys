# **Lebanese in the AI Era: Strategy v4.2**

*Cycle 4 — Phase B — Builder version, corrected following owner dispositions*

## **1. Purpose: two objectives, one project**

The project pursues two independently valuable objectives, supported by common infrastructure but evaluated against different measures of success.

**Objective 1 — AI that serves Lebanese speakers.** Make spoken Lebanese, including everyday mixing with French and English, Arabic script and Arabizi, usable in AI and digital services. Prioritize Lebanese-only speakers with limited economic or educational resources, followed by the diaspora. Researchers and AI businesses remain secondary beneficiaries.

The planning forecast is that large AI models will probably speak common Lebanese reasonably well within approximately two years. The project's comparative value therefore shifts towards independent evaluation, accessible services, adoption and documented, consented data rather than ownership of a Lebanese foundation model.

**Objective 2 — Cultural preservation.** Preserve and sustain regional vocabulary, oral arts, occupational speech, cultural contexts and intergenerational transmission. AI remains an optional supporting instrument. The owner's position that cultural flattening is irreversible remains a value judgment, not an established empirical finding.

Objective 2 has its own institutional host, cultural depositary, budget line and continuity plan. Closing an AI route or Objective 1 does not close Objective 2.

## **2. Design principles**

1. Adoption is a deliverable; K30 owns real use.
2. No retention before applicable gates pass except sealed rescue escrow (C-B, Annex E); no publication or licensing before clearance.
3. Public ASR and private synthesis have distinct consent and access regimes.
4. Withdrawal stops future project uses; removal from existing model weights or uncontrolled copies is not promised.
5. Wages precede speculative royalties.
6. Community authority never overrides individual rights.
7. Cultural preservation continues independently of AI performance, AI gates or commercial revenue.
8. Protect what the project controls; disclose cloning, imitation and redistribution risks without promising voice-identity protection.

## **3. Objective 1 — AI access, evaluation and adoption**

**K30 — Human-AI hybrid access network.** Retain two desks, a telephone line and two user-selected low-risk digital tasks. Compare human-only, human+AI and speech-first AI through randomized validation samples. Measure completion, overrides, errors and bail-outs; correct and re-sample. Evaluation recordings remain non-public unless separately contributed through P-A. Human-only assistance survives AI failure.

**P-A — Inclusive paid collection.** Prioritize elderly, rural and non-literate speakers. Pay recording, transcription and validation work.

|  | ASR | TTS / speech-to-speech |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Collection | Crowdsourced | Few selected voices |
| Environment | Natural ambient noise | Studio quality |
| Audio | Public after clearance | Private |
| Identity metadata | No names or identifying metadata | Pseudonyms |
| Models | Publication subject to gates | Never published |
| Consent | Explicit public release | Separate private use |

Individuals may contribute to both. Prohibit public cross-collection metadata linkage, but explain that anyone obtaining public ASR audio may use it to imitate the speaker's voice. Quarantine third-party speech and sensitive disclosures. Retain trained-facilitator oversight for diaspora contributions.

Pay through cash transfers or mobile wallets in available US dollars, with oral receipts for non-readers and a separate diaspora payment route. For both objectives, keep payment records separate from contribution identifiers, with restricted access and limited retention.

**K07 — Mixing and Arabizi tools.** Retain language identification, transliteration, normalization and ASR-first prototypes. Compare third-party systems before local fine-tuning. Private synthesis proceeds by owner decision; K30 needs and third-party comparisons determine voice numbers and task coverage.

**P-B — Independent evaluation.** Retain native-speaker panels, paid cultural red-teaming, quarterly controlled tests and rankings. Evaluate third-party and project models under identical conditions, including noise, regional variation and code-switching. Protect hidden tests. Apply Annex B's iterative correction framework and serious-harm rule.

**K21 — Research network.** Retain participatory research and shared tasks using public ASR and separately authorized documentation. Private synthesis is not a general research-access resource.

**K16 — Levantine hub.** Retain annotation alignment and separate Lebanese evaluation. Defer joint models and regional data pooling.

## **4. Objective 2 — Cultural preservation**

Four integrated programmes retain the complete selected portfolio.

### **Programme C-A — Living heritage and transmission**

*Combines C21, C25, C26, C27 and C29.*

- Record crafts, farming, occupational speech, humour, games and oral traditions with paid participation and community-defined boundaries.
- Fund year-long apprenticeships, paid tradition bearers, apprentice stipends and transparent selection.
- Support recurring zajal, storytelling, theatre and sung-poetry gatherings.
- Protect gathering places through municipal cooperation, accessible venues, permits and small grants.
- Encourage household circles using optional spoken prompts without recording or registration requirements. Provide alternatives for isolated elders.

**Measure:** sustained apprenticeships, continued practice, accessible gatherings and community-defined transmission outcomes.

### **Programme C-B — Broadcaster archive rescue and rights clearance**

*Combines C01, C03, C05 and C02, supporting C10.*

Inventory public, private, regional and discontinued broadcasters and independent collections. Document ownership, formats, condition and accessibility; connect existing initiatives.

Eligibility requires audible spoken Lebanese of cultural value, including everyday French/English mixing, with no minimum share. Priority follows carrier urgency, rarity and cultural value. Two listeners estimate language share using a fixed sample per programme (three two-minute excerpts). The archive lead decides, with appeal to the cultural council. Log every exclusion and its reason; review exclusions yearly. Preserve mixed programmes whole; catalogue Lebanese segments, retaining MSA segments in context but not as catalogue targets. Pure MSA remains outside scope.

Capture endangered carriers once during audit only with the holder’s written permission; seal the copy in escrow without access until rights and retention conditions are met. Language identification is an optional aid requiring human confirmation.

Prioritize endangered eligible recordings. Offer contractual preservation escrow without ownership transfer or compulsory publication. Pair paid regional preservation trainees with retired broadcaster technicians.

Retain four overlapping rights-triage categories, with the 50-year threshold provisional pending counsel confirmation before G-1 and cultural retention: broadcasts older than 50 years; broadcaster-owned younger material; third-party works or performers; unknown rights. Publish a non-sensitive rights register distinguishing clearance from unresolved claims.

**Measure:** eligible collections inventoried, endangered recordings preserved, rights cleared and independent technician competence.

### **Programme C-C — Mobile rescue, listening and circulation**

*Combines C04, C13 and C10.*

Operate a mobile rescue-and-listening unit. Return originals and copies of household-created content, subject to applicable rights. Until counsel’s M0–3 opinion on third-party copying, inventory household recordings of broadcasts only; do not digitize them. Retention requires separate consent and rights clearance.

Provide facilitated, solar-backed offline listening points in libraries, municipalities and community centres without literacy, registration or device requirements.

Re-broadcast cleared spoken-Lebanese programming. Broadcasting does not prevent unauthorized recording.

**Measure:** household materials rescued, listening-point availability, underserved communities reached and cleared programmes circulated.

### **Programme C-D — Community archives and linguistic diversity**

*Combines C09, C16 and C33.*

Establish community-governed regional repositories with shared preservation services, local access authority, independent appeals and external reviews.

Enable diaspora cataloguing through Arabic script, Arabizi and spoken annotations. Preserve spelling and regional variation.

Invite Armenian, Syriac, Aramaic, Kurdish, Domari and other communities to define preservation priorities. Provide Deaf-led visual preservation of Lebanese Sign Language.

Collections may be public, community-only, restricted or closed, including after transfer to the depositary (Annex E). Heritage collections never become AI training material by default.

**Measure:** community-approved collections, contextual catalogue quality, representation and effective community control.

## **5. Shared infrastructure and legal safeguards**

**K02 — Governance.** Legally capable hosts hold controller responsibility for their respective operations. Constitute interim community-majority governance at M0; the permanent AI council replaces its interim council at G-2. Constitute the permanent cultural council by M6 independently of AI gates. Quorum requires a majority of appointed seats, never fewer than three members, including a community majority. Legal and privacy vetoes remain absolute. Other disagreements suspend only the contested activity and must be resolved within 30 days; otherwise, including persistent lack of quorum, the independent reviewer decides.

**C35 — Consent.** Use oral-first, comprehension-tested modules for ASR publication, private synthesis, heritage, listening, broadcasting and research. Provide anonymous withdrawal credentials and accessible complaints; apply Annexes D and E for withdrawal, recovery and capacity safeguards.

**K22/K32 — Provenance and context.** Retain lineage, contextual envelopes and release validation. Separate public manifests from protected compliance records.

**Legal and operational safeguards:**

- Before any live recording for either objective, file Law 81/2018 declarations (including Article 96(12) for applicable foreign processing, backups or successors) and obtain research ethics committee approval. Article 98 makes declared transfers public. Require an Article 97 licence or exclusion of health data; genetic identity and sex-life data also require a licence if processed.
- Review diaspora privacy jurisdictions; GDPR obligations remain provisional pending counsel confirmation before G-1 and cultural retention. Retain applicable impact assessments and transfer safeguards.
- Verify secure storage at G-1; verify synthesis compute and associated access locations at G-3. Lebanese infrastructure remains the synthesis default.
- Obtain counsel's opinion on third-party copying in M0–3. Broadcaster rights, underlying works and performers require separate clearance; fifty years alone does not establish unrestricted reuse.
- Provide energy resilience, encrypted offline backups at a second site and manual outage procedures.

## **B. Iterative validation and serious-harm rule**

Compare both AI routes against randomized human-only assistance on selected tasks. Sample Lebanese-only, non-literate, low-income, rural, elderly and phone-only users, including intersections.

Reference targets—not pass/fail criteria:

| Indicator | Reference target |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Completion | At least 80% |
| AI–human completion difference | At least −5 percentage points |
| Excess errors | No more than 3 percentage points |
| Excess re-contacts | No more than 3 percentage points |
| Human+AI completion time | Shorter than human-only |
| Repeat use | At least 40% within 60 days, only for tasks recurring within that period |
| Five leading remediable failures | Address all; reduce recurrence by 20% |
| Bail-outs | Count as AI non-completion; record predefined minimal overrides separately |

Validate continuously; serious-harm rule applies throughout.

Report sample sizes, differences and uncertainty; investigate disparities and document corrections. Inconclusive observations are not failures. No confirmatory power, familywise-error or mandatory subgroup significance requirements.

One serious attributable privacy or task-related harm triggers immediate suspension and independent investigation. Human-only support continues. Repeated shortfalls require correction, not automatic closure.

## **C. Access and release contract**

Removing names does not anonymize public ASR. Publish cleared audio, transcripts, non-identifying context and purpose-specific terms. Exclude identifiable third-party material and hidden benchmarks. Registration-free downloads.

Private synthesis tiers:

- Tier 0: safe documentation and aggregates.
- Tier 1: restricted internal derivatives and embeddings.
- Tier 2: private recordings, training environments and models; authorized personnel only; no external audio or model publication.

Record opaque identifiers, lineage, asset class, consent version, purposes, context, annotation versions, script, restrictions and withdrawal status. Derivatives inherit restrictive permissions. Unknown origin, conflicting permissions, withdrawn sources and test/train overlap block release. JSON validation precedes PROV-O mapping.

## **D. Quarantine, withdrawal and appeals**

Mute separable incidental speech; purge originals within 14 days after quality assurance. Hold identifiable bystanders encrypted for at most 30 days; re-consent, mute or purge. Scrub sensitive disclosures within 14 days or purge. Immediately purge unconsented minors or evident distress.

Independent appeals within seven days cannot extend quarantine or delay deletion.

Acknowledge withdrawal within seven days; immediately block future project uses of the withdrawn contribution. Retire withdrawn private-synthesis voices from all project use, including K30 service, within 30 days. Remove controlled source copies within 30 days unless lawful retention is necessary. Notify contracted recipients; require deletion attestations within 30 days.

Consent explains: existing weights need not be erased; voice identity may remain reproducible; public copies may persist or enable imitation. No voice-identity removal or segment-exclusion mechanism promised.

Publish a non-identifying withdrawal list: affected record identifiers, deprecated releases and checksums. Consent and terms specify synchronization as a good-faith obligation unenforceable against anonymous downloaders. Optional sealed contact records with the privacy officer enable credential recovery; no voice matching.

Without AI renewal, cease new collection and licensing; securely delete private synthesis assets, quarantine, service logs and test sets, retaining only necessary compliance evidence and residual public-ASR governance.

## **E. Cultural gates**

Before live recording: filed Law 81/2018 heritage declaration, including applicable Art. 96(12) transfers (foreign depositary or backups), and research ethics committee approval. Before retention: contracted depositary, consent, eligibility, community authority, preservation rights, S9–S10 counsel confirmation, Art. 97 licence or exclusion of health data (genetic identity and sex-life data also require licensing). Only holder-authorized sealed audit escrow may precede rights clearance (C-B). Separate clearance precedes listening, online access or broadcast.

Failed comprehension or doubtful capacity excludes public ASR; trusted-companion heritage participation requires lawful consent.

Annually review governance, preservation integrity and transmission. Failure stops only affected operations. Without renewal, the closing operation ceases new collection and licensing. Transfer heritage under existing consents and community access rules (public, community-only, restricted, closed); depositary is custodian, not access authority.
